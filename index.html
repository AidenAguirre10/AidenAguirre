About

I'm an industrial designer based in the San Francisco Bay Area with a background in Color, Materials, and Finish (CMF) design. My design identity is grounded in CMF, and I'm working toward a career as an exterior or interior automotive designer. Transportation design is the ultimate design goal.

Alongside client and portfolio work, I freelance on product rendering and CMF projects, and I keep building studio skills through coursework in transportation, furniture, and soft goods design.
